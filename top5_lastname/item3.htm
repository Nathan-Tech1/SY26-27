Benihana

Benihana transforms dining into an engaging theatrical experience by having highly skilled teppanyaki chefs prepare meals right before your eyes with impressive knife flips and fiery tricks. The menu delivers consistently delicious, classic flavors, highlighted by their deeply craveable hibachi chicken rice and signature dipping sauces. Ultimately, the interactive, communal seating layout makes it the perfect destination for celebrating milestones and creating lasting memories with friends and family.
